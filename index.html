SECTION A

Read the Actual Work: A Sample 3-Touch Sequence

No vague promises — here is exactly the kind of sequence we write and send. Everything below is a sample: the sender, the prospect and the company are fictional, created only to show the work.

Sample — Fictional Companies
TOUCH 1 · DAY 1
From: Alex at SummitClean Partners (SAMPLE) → Facilities Manager, mid-size office park (SAMPLE prospect)
Subject: Cleaning crews for the night shift?

Hi [First Name] — noticed [Company] runs about 40,000 sq ft across two buildings. Most facilities managers I talk to in [City] are quietly unhappy with their night crew: missed areas, no inspection reports, and a supervisor nobody can reach.

SummitClean (sample) cleans 30+ commercial buildings in [Territory] with a documented nightly checklist your team can actually see. Worth a 15-minute look at how we'd handle your buildings — with a fixed quote, no walkthrough marathon?

— Alex, SummitClean Partners (SAMPLE) · Reply STOP to opt out

TOUCH 2 · DAY 4 — SAME THREAD
Subject: Re: Cleaning crews for the night shift?

Quick follow-up, [First Name]. The two things that usually trigger a switch: (1) tenants complaining before you hear about it, and (2) paying for deep-clean items that never actually happen.

We include a monthly inspection report with photos, so you see the work instead of trusting it. If your current crew already sends that, ignore me. If not — 15 minutes this week or next?

— Alex (SAMPLE)

TOUCH 3 · DAY 9 — THE BREAK-UP
Subject: Should I close the file?

[First Name] — I've reached out a couple of times about [Company]'s cleaning contract and haven't heard back, so I'll close the file on my end.

If the contract comes up for renewal this year and you want a second quote to keep your current vendor honest, reply with “quote” and I'll send our one-page pricing format. Either way, no hard feelings.

— Alex (SAMPLE)

Every sequence is written per client, per industry — this sample uses commercial cleaning; roofing, HVAC, agencies and software get their own angles, proof points and objections.
